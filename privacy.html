Privacy Policy

Effective date: April 2026 · Revella, Vancouver, BC, Canada

Revella is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights under British Columbia's Personal Information Protection Act (PIPA) and Canada's Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA). By using Revella, you consent to the collection, use, and disclosure of your personal information as described in this policy.

1. Who we are

Revella is a compatibility-first dating application currently operating in Vancouver, British Columbia. For privacy inquiries, contact us at privacy@revella.ca.

2. What information we collect

We collect the following categories of personal information:

  • Account information — your name, email address, age, and gender
  • Intake answers — your responses to the 40-question compatibility intake
  • Photos — your profile photos and verification selfie
  • Usage data — how you interact with the app, including match activity and message timestamps
  • Communications — messages sent between matched users within the app
  • Reports — any reports you submit about other users
  • Consent records — the date and time you agreed to this Privacy Policy

3. Why we collect it

We collect your information only for the following purposes:

  • To create and manage your account
  • To match you with compatible users based on your intake answers
  • To facilitate blind voice calls and photo reveals between matched users
  • To verify your identity through manual review of your verification selfie
  • To enable in-app messaging between users who have mutually consented to connect
  • To review reports of misconduct and maintain platform safety
  • To communicate with you about your account and matches
  • To improve the matching process and platform over time

4. How your information is used

Your intake answers are used solely for matching and are never shared with other users — not before, during, or after a Pod. Your photos are stored privately and are only shared with a matched user after both parties have given explicit consent to the reveal. Your verification selfie is reviewed only by the Revella team and is never shown to other users.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to any third party. We do not use your information for advertising purposes.

5. Who has access to your information

Access to your personal information is limited to:

  • The Revella team, for matching, verification, and safety review purposes
  • Your matched user, but only the information you have explicitly consented to share — your first name and photos after mutual reveal consent
  • Supabase — database and file storage, hosted in the United States
  • Vercel — application hosting
  • Daily.co — real-time audio infrastructure for Pod calls. Pods are not recorded. No audio is stored by Revella or Daily.co after a call ends
  • Gmail — used to send transactional emails such as match notifications and Pod reminders

All third-party service providers are required to handle your data in accordance with applicable privacy laws.

6. Photos, calls, and sensitive information

Your profile photos are stored in a private storage environment and are only accessible via secure, time-limited signed URLs. They are never publicly accessible. Your verification selfie is stored separately and is only accessible to the Revella team.

Photos are shared with a matched user only when both users have independently and explicitly consented to the reveal. If either user declines, no photos are shared and neither user is informed of the other's decision.

Pods — your blind voice calls — are not recorded. No audio is captured, stored, or reviewed by Revella or any third party. What you say in a Pod stays in a Pod.

In-app messages are stored securely and are not routinely read by our team. In the event of a safety report or conduct violation, messages may be reviewed as part of our investigation process.

7. Data retention

We retain your personal information for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, your personal data is permanently removed within 30 days, except where retention is required by law or for legitimate safety purposes such as unresolved misconduct reports or active investigations.

In-app messages between matched users are automatically deleted after 90 days of inactivity in that match. If the match remains active, the retention period resets.

Your verification selfie is retained for 90 days after your account is deleted and then permanently removed. In cases involving confirmed misconduct or bans, verification photos may be retained indefinitely as part of the safety record.

Banned account records — including email address and ban reason — are retained indefinitely to prevent re-registration.

8. Your rights under PIPA and PIPEDA

As a resident of British Columbia, you have the following rights regarding your personal information:

  • The right to know what personal information we hold about you
  • The right to access your personal information upon request
  • The right to correct inaccurate or incomplete information
  • The right to withdraw consent to the collection, use, or disclosure of your information, subject to legal and contractual restrictions
  • The right to request deletion of your personal information
  • The right to file a complaint with the Office of the Information and Privacy Commissioner for British Columbia (OIPC BC)

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at privacy@revella.ca. We will respond within 30 days.

9. Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, disclosure, or loss. This includes encrypted storage, private photo buckets with signed URL access, and restricted internal access to sensitive data.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. If you have reason to believe your information has been compromised, contact us immediately at privacy@revella.ca.

10. Cookies and tracking

Revella does not use advertising cookies or third-party tracking tools. We may use essential session cookies required for authentication. We do not track your activity across other websites or applications.

11. Children

Revella is intended for users aged 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If we become aware that a user is under 18, their account will be removed immediately and their data deleted.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the effective date at the top of this page and notify active users by email. Continued use of Revella after changes are posted constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.

13. Contact

For any privacy-related questions, requests, or complaints, contact us at privacy@revella.ca.

If you are not satisfied with our response, you may contact the Office of the Information and Privacy Commissioner for British Columbia at oipc.bc.ca.
