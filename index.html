Revella is a dating app designed to prioritize compatibility and real connection before appearance. Instead of swiping through photos, you are matched based on shared values and lifestyle alignment, then invited to a 20-minute blind voice call with cameras off and no photos. After the call, if both people want to continue, photos are revealed and introductions are made. If either person declines, the match ends privately.

The intake is what makes matching meaningful. Most dating apps match people based on photos and minimal profile information. Revella uses a 40-question intake to understand your values, lifestyle, relationship patterns, and what you're actually looking for, so matches are based on compatibility instead of guesswork.
Most users complete it in 5 to 15 minutes.
Revella uses your answers internally solely for matching. Your matches do not see your intake responses before, during, or after a Pod.
Revella first applies your non-negotiables, then matches you based on deeper compatibility signals such as lifestyle fit, values, and relationship intent. During the beta, matches are reviewed to ensure quality.
Timing depends on your filters and the local user pool. Most beta users receive a match within 1 to 2 weeks after completing the intake.
A Pod is a scheduled 20-minute blind voice call between two matched users. Cameras are off and photos are not shared beforehand. After the call, both users privately decide whether to continue.
If both people choose to continue, photos are shared and an introduction is made. If either person declines, the match ends privately and respectfully.
